01 / MECHANICAL DESIGN · ROBOTICS

Prosthetic robotic hand

From a digital assembly to a working mechanism.

Project index ↑

A personal project exploring the design, fabrication, and actuation of a 3D-printed prosthetic robotic hand.

  • CAD & assembly
  • 3D printing
  • Actuation

Engineering focus

Connecting mechanical design with hands-on fabrication and control. This case study will document the assembly, design iterations, and grasp demonstrations.

Documentation to develop

Add measured range of motion, repeatability, grip testing, and the design changes informed by those results as testing progresses.

02 / GRADUATE RESEARCH · COMPUTATIONAL MODELING

Cardiovascular simulation

Exploring physiology through a computational model.

Project index ↑

My graduate project at UC Davis: a Julia-based lumped-parameter cardiovascular model for exploring the behavior of the circulatory system.

  • Julia
  • ModelingToolkit
  • Physiological modeling

Engineering focus

Representing cardiovascular subsystems and their interactions in a modular simulation. The case study will explain the model architecture, assumptions, and selected simulation conditions.

Documentation to develop

Pair each graph with the simulation condition, labeled axes and units, and a short interpretation. Include validation methods and model limitations alongside the results.

03 / BIOMEDICAL INSTRUMENTATION · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Holter monitor

Following the signal from acquisition to visualization.

Project index ↑

A personal ECG monitoring prototype exploring signal acquisition, embedded electronics, data recording, and waveform visualization.

  • ECG acquisition
  • Embedded electronics
  • Signal visualization

Engineering focus

Bringing sensing, electronics, and packaging into one prototype. The case study will trace the signal path and explain how the components fit inside the enclosure.

Documentation to develop

Document signal quality, sampling settings, recording duration, and power measurements as they are verified. Explain how the displayed ECG is scaled and processed.

Educational prototype. Not validated for clinical use.
